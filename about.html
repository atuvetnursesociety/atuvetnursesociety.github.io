About us

We're the student society for everyone studying veterinary nursing, a place to get more hands-on experience, meet people across the years, and have some fun outside of lectures.

Who we are

The society is run by a small student committee, elected each year, with support from staff in the department. Membership is open to all vet nursing students at ATU VNS. See the committee page to find out who's involved this year.

What we do

Talks

Guest speakers from clinics, charities and industry throughout the year. See the events page for what's coming up.

Clinic & farm visits

Organised trips to see practice in real settings, building confidence before placement.

Socials

Quiz nights, seasonal socials and casual meetups: a chance to get to know people outside class.

Fundraisers

Bake sales and events in aid of local animal charities and shelters.
